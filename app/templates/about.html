About Instagram Saved Posts Organizer

📱 What is this?

A web application that helps you extract, organize, and search your Instagram saved posts. Instead of scrolling through hundreds of saved posts on Instagram, you can organize them with categories, tags, and powerful search.

✨ Features

  • 📥 Extract all your saved Instagram posts
  • 🤖 AI-powered automatic categorization suggestions
  • 🏷️ Manual tagging and organization
  • 🔍 Full-text search across captions and hashtags
  • 📊 Export your organized collection
  • 💾 Everything stored locally on your machine

🔒 Privacy

All your data is stored locally on your computer. We don't send your posts or credentials to any external servers (except when using OpenAI for AI categorization, which is optional).

🛠️ Technology

  • Backend: Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy
  • Database: SQLite with FTS5
  • Instagram: Instaloader
  • AI: OpenAI API / Local models (CLIP)
